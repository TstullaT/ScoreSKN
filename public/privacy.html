Privacy Policy for SCORESKN

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to SCORESKN. This Privacy Policy describes how SCORESKN ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our web and mobile applications for live sports statistics and championship schedules in St. Kitts and Nevis.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

SCORESKN is designed as a sports reference and live information hub. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifying information such as your name, phone number, physical address, or financial information.

2. Local Device Storage

To provide a fast and personalized experience, SCORESKN may save your theme preferences (e.g. Light/Dark mode), selected league filters, and local cached fixture views directly in your device's browser local storage. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Data & Network Requests

To display live standings, scores, and schedules, SCORESKN fetches public match data and public fixture spreadsheets. Standard web connection information (such as IP address and user-agent) is transmitted solely to deliver requested data over encrypted HTTPS.

4. Children’s Privacy

SCORESKN does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application provides general public sports information suitable for all ages.

5. Google Play & Android Compliance

SCORESKN complies with Google Play Developer Program Policies. The app does not access contacts, location sensors, cameras, microphones, or external storage without explicit user interaction.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect service updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL.

7. Contact Us
